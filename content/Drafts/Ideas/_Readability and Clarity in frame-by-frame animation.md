I'm a believer that in animation plays a more vital role than geometry (or anatomy, light and perspective which are all just part of geometry anyway).

As long as the movement is 'sold' well you can get away with a lot more than you might think. The human brain notices when something is wrong on an intuitive level, but it is also quite easy to fool if you know what to pay attention to.
